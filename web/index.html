01 · Problem

Problem

Operating thousands of AI services and multi-marketplace Customer Service workflows exposes compliance, fairness, and policy-consistency gaps that manual review questionnaires cannot catch in time.

Fleet Governance & CS Knowledge

Overview

ResponsibleAIRegistry automates four governance and content gates before an AI workload or policy update reaches customers. First, Python AST and CloudFormation template scanners inventory foundation-model calls and reconcile them against a SQLite WAL AI Bill of Materials (AI-BOM) in 0.5 ms median across 12 microservice repositories. Second, a hybrid policy engine and Amazon Bedrock classifier map systems to EU AI Act and NIST AI RMF tiers in 0.021 ms deterministic gate time. Third, a statistical fairness engine evaluates 4,000 subgroup decisions and tunes per-group thresholds in 30.617 ms median to satisfy the Four-Fifths (0.80) disparate impact rule. Fourth, a write-time compiler on Amazon Bedrock (us.amazon.nova-lite-v1:0) distills Customer Service SOPs across en-US, es-US, de-DE, and ja-JP into verified JSON Knowledge Artifacts with 100.0% numeric precision and exception recall.

01 · SHADOW AI DRIFT

Unregistered Models & Missing Guardrails

Builder teams add bedrock-runtime, sagemaker-runtime, or external openai SDK calls directly in feature branches, or redeploy registered bots to EU regions without guardrails or S3 retention limits.

02 · REGULATORY EXPOSURE

Manual EU AI Act & NIST RMF Triage

High-risk hiring, credit underwriting, and medical triage workflows require Annex IV documentation, fairness audits, and Article 14 human override before CI/CD pipelines permit production release.

03 · SUBGROUP DISPARITY

Uniform Cutoff Scores Penalize Cohorts

Applying a single 0.50 probability threshold across uncalibrated score distributions produces severe disparate impact (0.5523 DI) and true-positive gaps (0.3188) across protected cohorts.

04 · LOCALE CONTRADICTIONS

Raw Prose SOPs Drift Across Marketplaces

Stuffing verbose Customer Service prose into prompts wastes 50.0% of policy tokens while hiding stale es-US translations (45 vs 30 days) and invalid 7-day de-DE withdrawal rules.

03 · Implementation

Implementation

Five Python 3.12 modules inside rai_registry/ implement AST and CloudFormation discovery, regulatory risk gating, statistical fairness optimization, write-time SOP distillation, and unified local or Lambda serving.

12 / 12 Unit Tests Passing
01 · SHADOW-AI AST & IAC SCANNER rai_registry/shadow_ai_bom.py
Automatically inventories every foundation-model call, third-party AI SDK, system prompt hash, agent tool binding, and storage sink across code repositories and CloudFormation templates, flagging unregistered services and model or region drift.
_PythonAIBomVisitor(ast.NodeVisitor) inspects converse, invoke_model, invoke_endpoint, and outbound SDKs (openai, anthropic, api.deepseek.com) while _inspect_iac_template() checks AWS::Bedrock::Guardrail, S3 ExpirationInDays, DynamoDB TTL, and wildcard IAM roles against approved_ai_bom in 0.5 ms median (0.878 ms p95).
02 · EU AI ACT & NIST RMF CONFORMANCE GATE rai_registry/risk_conformance.py
Maps AI workloads into PROHIBITED, HIGH_RISK, TRANSPARENCY_LIMITED, and MINIMAL_RISK tiers, builds structured Model Cards, and blocks CI/CD promotion whenever mandatory controls are missing.
classify_ai_system_risk() runs deterministic policy rules in 0.021 ms median alongside Amazon Bedrock semantic review, combining both through a max-severity lattice so High-Risk Annex III workloads (hiring, credit, medical triage, critical infrastructure, biometrics) achieve 100.0% recall across all 16 golden benchmark systems.
03 · SUBGROUP FAIRNESS & THRESHOLD MITIGATOR rai_registry/fairness_metrics.py
Computes exact confusion matrices across demographic or operational cohorts, evaluates the EEOC Four-Fifths (0.80) Disparate Impact rule, Equal Opportunity (|ΔTPR|), Equalized Odds, and 10-bin Expected Calibration Error (ECE).
optimize_group_thresholds() searches cohort-specific decision cutoffs across 4,000 rows in 30.617 ms median (44.538 ms p95), lifting resume-screening Disparate Impact from 0.5523 to 0.9069 and cutting |ΔTPR| from 0.3188 to 0.0437 across 3 / 3 benchmark scenarios.
04 · VERIFIED CS KNOWLEDGE ARTIFACT DISTILLER rai_registry/sop_artifact_distiller.py · server.py
Compiles multi-page Customer Service SOP prose into compact, schema-typed JSON Knowledge Artifacts at publish time and catches policy contradictions across en-US, es-US, de-DE, and ja-JP before agents serve stale rules.
distill_policy_bundle() invokes Amazon Bedrock (us.amazon.nova-lite-v1:0, 623.0 ms median across 13 calls), enforces verify_artifact_entailment() to reject ungrounded numbers (100.0% numeric precision and exception recall, 50.0% token reduction), and flags 5 cross-locale conflicts.
04 · Trade-Offs

Trade-Offs

Every architectural choice balances regulatory certainty, latency, and serverless cost efficiency against static-analysis limits or retraining complexity.

Design Decisions
01 · STATIC AST + IAC VS RUNTIME EBPF

Pre-Merge CI Discovery Instead of Kernel Packet Inspection

Parsing Python ASTs and CloudFormation JSON catches shadow SDK imports, hardcoded endpoints, and missing guardrails in 0.5 ms median before deployment without agent overhead, with the trade-off that dynamically constructed URLs loaded from encrypted secrets at runtime still require VPC egress logs.

02 · MAX-SEVERITY LATTICE VS PURE LLM

Zero High-Risk False Negatives Over Permissive Triage

Combining deterministic policy rules with Amazon Bedrock via max_severity(det_tier, bedrock_tier) guarantees that persuasive PRD wording cannot downgrade an Annex III hiring or credit system, trading a slightly higher Legal review queue rate on borderline text for 100.0% High-Risk recall.

03 · POST-PROCESSING THRESHOLDS VS RETRAINING

30.6 ms Threshold Calibration Instead of Multi-Hour Retraining

Optimizing cohort decision cutoffs restores Four-Fifths compliance (0.5523 -> 0.9069 DI) in 30.617 ms on black-box model scores without touching upstream weights, though long-term feature drift still benefits from periodic in-processing sample reweighting.

04 · WRITE-TIME ARTIFACTS VS CHUNK RAG

Upfront Publish Compilation for Faster, Conflict-Free Serving

Distilling SOPs into atomic Knowledge Artifacts adds a few seconds of Bedrock compilation when an author edits a policy, but cuts serving-time prompt tokens by 50.0%, guarantees 100.0% numeric grounding, and blocks cross-locale contradictions before customers ever see them.
